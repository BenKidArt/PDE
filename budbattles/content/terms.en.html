Terms of participation (draft)

Draft – please review and adjust before launch. The German version is binding.

1. Organiser

BudBattles is a free photo competition. Nothing is sold. Details about the organiser are in the legal notice (Impressum).

2. Who can take part

Only adults (18+) with a confirmed email address. One account per person.

3. Photos

  • Each person may enter one photo per battle.
  • The photo must be your own and show a legal home grow under the German Cannabis Act (KCanG).
  • Not allowed: people, addresses, licence plates, offers to sell or swap, advertising.
  • Every photo is reviewed before it is published and may be rejected without giving reasons.
  • Location data (GPS) and other metadata are removed automatically.

4. Usage rights

You allow BudBattles to show your photo with your display name as part of the competition. You can withdraw your photo while entries are open.

5. Voting

Each member has one vote per battle. You cannot vote for your own photo. The photo with the most votes gets featured on BudBattles. Attempts to manipulate votes lead to exclusion.

6. Privacy

Details are in the privacy policy.
